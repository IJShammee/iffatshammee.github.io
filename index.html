About

I am a dedicated water resources researcher specializing in hydrological and hydraulic modeling, groundwater management, and climate resilience. Currently pursuing my MSc in Water Resource Development at BUET's Institute of Water and Flood Management, my research focuses on innovative solutions for sustainable water management, including Managed Aquifer Recharge (MAR) and Aquifer Storage and Recovery (ASR) systems. With expertise in advanced modeling tools like MODFLOW, HEC-RAS, and geospatial analysis platforms, I aim to contribute to evidence-based water resource management strategies that address the challenges of climate change and urbanization in Bangladesh and beyond.
